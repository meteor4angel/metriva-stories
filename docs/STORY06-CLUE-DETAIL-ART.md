# Story 6 clue-detail artwork

These clue images were created with the built-in image-generation tool from the approved realistic scene artwork. Each detail keeps the same object, material, location, and lighting as its wide scene. The game displays the detail in the evidence window and enlarges that same image in the zoom window. The wide scene remains the clickable investigation backdrop.

The common image-generation direction was: create a realistic, front-readable close inspection of the named clue within the supplied wide scene. Preserve the approved scene's object identity, environment, material, weather, warm lantern and cool storm lighting. Show the clue alone as the primary subject, facing the player when it contains writing or a diagram. Render the exact story information listed below at legible size. Do not reuse the full wide-scene view as the detail and do not add unrelated objects.

- **Valve chart:** East Sluice → Orchard Stair → Aqueduct Bend → Springworks, with Springworks circled in blue pencil.
- **Ferry key ledger:** Lower sluice key issued to Marit Pell at 8:40 and returned at 8:53, with the return in visibly different ink; show the key beside the open book.
- **Pipe diagram:** Tower Footing, Aqueduct Bend, Narrow Pipe, and Stone Resonator as a connected maintenance drawing.
- **Echo Keeper verse:** “When spring is bound and narrow made,  the quiet stone will sing.  Unbind the path beneath the roots,  and let the flood go wing.”
- **Four verse stones:** “Face the first light”; “where the reed roots drink”; “below the third arch”; “the spring door waits.”
- **Spring-garden map:** Clearly identify the east-facing entrance stone, reed bed, old well, three arches, and stair descending under the third arch.
- **Counterweight mark:** A downward chalk arrow beside the door and Edda's warning, “Don't lift against the water. Release the weight after the bypass is open.”

Chapter 5 detail artwork is reserved for the later pass.
