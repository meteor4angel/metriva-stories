# Story 6 Chapter 4 — Garden route

The canonical puzzle is `verse-route` in `stories/story-06.json`. Its `answer` remains `east → reeds → third-arch → spring-door`; the shared Story Engine renders `presentation.mode: "garden-route"` as an interactive map. The verse panel deliberately presents the four fragments out of answer order.

`assets/story06-garden-map-puzzle.png` is a route-free derivative of the existing spring-garden survey. It preserves the eastern stones, reed bed, old well, three arches, stair, and spring door. The same route-free map is used for the garden-map evidence detail so inspecting the clue does not display a painted solution. The previous detail image remains in the project for provenance but is no longer referenced by the game.

Coordinates in `presentation.locations` use the map's intrinsic 1672 × 941 coordinate system. The image, markers, and SVG route paths share one viewBox; responsive resizing and zoom preserve their alignment. The Third Arch and Spring Door hotspots sit on their pictured landmarks and have nonoverlapping touch areas. The engine adds markers and route lines at runtime rather than baking them into the image. Clicking a selected landmark removes it; Undo removes the final step. The board labels the numbered trail **Your selected order** while keeping the verse fragments shuffled. Proposed paths stay antique gold until the correct complete route is submitted, when a brief emerald confirmation plays. Partially selected routes are saved in `puzzleRouteSelections` under `verse-route`, consistent with Chapter 2's route renderer. Opening either evidence clue uses a stacked dialog; closing it returns to the mounted route without changing zoom, pan, selection, or focus context.

The Chapter 4 gate still requires its four evidence items and the Jori/Sella conversations. Solving the route triggers the existing chapter-solved event. It identifies the spring door and leaves the bypass and counterweight mechanism for the next investigation.
