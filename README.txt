METRIVA STORIES v0.7.5 — EVELYN VOICE UPGRADE

Extract the whole ZIP and open index.html in Chrome or Edge. Choose
Echoes in the Library. The completed Story 01 build is also included.

Story 02 has distinct character voices and a player voice. Dialogue
plays when you open or advance a spoken line. If the browser asks for
interaction, select Play voice. Mute voices enables quiet reading;
Replay voice repeats the current line. Advancing or closing stops the
old voice. Pausing motion leaves audio available. All audio is local.

Progress, transcripts, five chapters, puzzles and endings are retained.
Export a save before moving the folder. Import and Restart affect only
Story 02. See TEST-RESULTS.md and STORY-PATTERN.md.

To replace a voice, open AUDIO-SCRIPTS.csv, find the exact line, then
overwrite its clearly named MP3 in assets/voices/. Keep the filename.
The player waits one second before it starts the MP3 and one second
after the MP3 finishes. Replacement recordings need no added silence.

screenshots-voices/ contains the voice-controls screenshots.
screenshots-story02/ contains the earlier gameplay captures; its GIF
demonstrates visual animation and does not contain audio.
