# Story 05 voice direction

The speaking cast is **Mira** and **Theo**. The librarian and garden coordinator remain unnamed background characters; they have no dialogue.

Mira: a young adult with a natural contemporary American or lightly American-accented voice. Warm, curious, quick to smile, quietly confident. Medium pitch. Avoid a breathy or childlike delivery.

Theo: a young adult with a natural contemporary American or lightly American-accented voice. Gentle, grounded, relaxed, with an easy smile and a little vulnerability. Medium-low pitch, clear and unforced. Avoid a booming announcer voice.

Record only the quoted spoken lines from `js/days-dialogue.js`; the `action` fields are visual narration. Begin each clip with a short clean lead-in so the first words remain audible on replay. Keep the wording exact. Audition one introduction from each character before generating all lines, as with earlier stories. No voice asset is assigned until its sample is approved.

Artwork was made with the built-in image generation tool. Portrait prompts used the approved modern Story 05 cover as an identity reference and requested four upper-body expressions for each lead. Clue prompts requested five distinct contemporary objects per chapter. Scene prompts requested the bookshop, rainy bakery, garden fundraiser, and riverside terrace in a bright modern visual-novel style.
