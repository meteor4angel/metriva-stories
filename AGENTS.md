# Clue artwork continuity

For future changes to investigation scenes and clue images in any story, establish one authoritative master composition first. Derive the wide scene and each clue detail from that composition or its source artwork. A clue detail may use a higher-resolution crop or carefully placed text overlay so essential writing is readable, while the wide scene only needs to show the same physical object and arrangement.

Keep furniture, props, note positions, page layout, handwriting, deletion marks, and lighting consistent across the wide scene and its details. Do not independently regenerate each view. Place clues naturally and fully supported by the depicted surface. Check the wide scene and every corresponding detail side by side before finishing; preserve clue IDs, markers, text, and gameplay unless the user specifically asks to change them.
