REPLACING STORY 02 AUDIO

Open AUDIO-SCRIPTS.csv to find each filename and its exact dialogue.
Export your replacement as MP3 and overwrite the matching file in
assets/voices/. Keep the filename unchanged.

Example: 01-reading-evelyn-introduction.mp3 is Evelyn's opening speech.
Files ending in answer or follow-up identify the corresponding question.
Files containing player are your spoken questions or follow-up lines.

The player adds one second before speech and one second after it.
You do not need to add silence to your replacement recordings.
Refresh the page after replacing a file. Progress stays in the browser.
