# Approved Metriva Stories pattern

User approved Story 01 v0.6.1 on 14 October 2026. Apply this pattern to subsequent stories.

- Warm painterly anime art, cream paper panels, forest green accents and golden sunlight.
- Separate character sprites over the appropriate chapter room; no source-board portrait crops or oversized unrelated characters in backgrounds.
- Blink, listening, speaking and thoughtful expressions, gentle movement, pause and reduced-motion support.
- Spoken dialogue with distinct character voices. Bundle audio locally; provide mute and replay controls. Animate mouths only during actual audible character playback. Quiet mode keeps blinking and expressions without speaking movement. Stop the old line when advancing or closing a conversation.
- Conversations advance through player questions, character replies and follow-up turns. Save the current line and transcript.
- Five distinct chapters, readable clue illustrations and text, meaningful puzzles, gentle hints and clear chapter gates.
- Browser progress, export/import, restart only current story, verified mobile layout and both endings.
- Preserve completed stories when upgrading another. Deliver a self-contained tested ZIP with real screenshots.
